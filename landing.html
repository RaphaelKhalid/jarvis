What's inside

A real lab, not a toy diagram.

Every part behaves the way its real-world twin does. Wire it wrong and you'll see the short — wire it right and it comes alive.

🔌

A real circuit solver

Modified Nodal Analysis under the hood. Batteries and motors are true voltage sources, LEDs are nonlinear diodes, and it flags shorts and over-current live as you wire.

🧩

Tons of components + search

Batteries, motors, resistors, switches, LEDs, potentiometers and more — each with real pins and parameters. Find any part fast and drop it straight onto the bench.

🤖

Jarvis, your AI co-builder

Describe what you want in plain language. Jarvis places components, wires the pins, and tunes parameters for you — then explains what it did.

🎢

Physics simulation on RUN

Hit RUN and the solved motor current becomes real torque in a Rapier physics world. Watch your build actually move, spin, and settle under gravity.

📱

Runs in the browser

No downloads, no toolchains, no drivers. WebGL and WebAssembly do the heavy lifting, so it's smooth on a laptop and touch-friendly on iPad and iPhone.

⚡

Live feedback everywhere

Charge visibly flows along wires at solved speed, LEDs glow in proportion to current, and a powered motor's wheel spins — all before you enter the sim.

How it works

From empty bench to running robot in three steps.

1

Place your parts

Drag components from the tray onto the bench. They fall, collide, and settle with real gravity, just like the real thing.

2

Wire the circuit

Click pin to pin to connect. The solver runs instantly — LEDs light, motors throttle, and shorts glow red. Or just ask Jarvis to wire it.

3

Hit RUN

Enter the physics sim and drive the motor your circuit powers. Tune a value, re-run, and see the difference immediately.
