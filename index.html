SelfBalance Lab

Build a self-balancing robot, then drive it.
  1. 1
    Assemble

    Drag the six parts onto the chassis. Hover a part’s ? to see what it does.

  2. 2
    Wire

    Click a pin, then click its target to connect them. Hover a pin to see what it is (what’s IN2?), or hit Auto-wire to do it for you.

  3. 3
    Program

    Tune the PID gains (Kp, Ki, Kd) in the Arduino sketch on the right. Hover any keyword to see which physical part it controls.

  4. 4
    Run

    Hit Upload and drive with W A S D. The robot balances as it moves.
